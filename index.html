Fair questions

What is TrackWarranty?

TrackWarranty is a free warranty tracker and bill keeper app. You snap a bill or receipt, it reads the product, store, purchase date and warranty length, and reminds you 30, 7 and 1 day before the warranty expires. It also builds a one-tap claim kit with the invoice and serial number. It works offline, saves to your phone first, needs no sign-up, and supports any country and currency. It is available on Android, with iOS coming soon.

Is it really free?

Yes. Tracking, reminders and claim kits are free.

Do I need an account?

No. Start offline straight away and type in the details, or sign in for free to have bills read automatically and to back up and sync your vault.

Can I share it with my family?

Yes. Create a household in the app and share the invite code. Everyone sees the bills you share, and each person can still keep private items. Sharing needs a free sign-in.

Can I export my data?

Yes. Export your whole vault as a CSV spreadsheet, a PDF report with receipt photos, or a full backup ZIP with every receipt file. It works offline.

What if I lose my phone?

Turn on backup and your vault restores on any new phone when you sign in.

Does it work in my country?

Yes. It works with any currency and your local date and number formats. Warranties are a household problem everywhere.

I'm a brand. Can I use this for my products?

Yes. TrackWarranty for Brands lets you register buyers with a QR on the box and run your own warranty programs.
