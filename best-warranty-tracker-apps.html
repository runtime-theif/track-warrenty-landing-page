What to look for in a warranty tracker

  • Fast capture: snap a bill and have the details filled in for you.
  • Reminders before expiry, not just storage.
  • Works offline and keeps a copy on your phone, so it opens when you need it.
  • Easy claims: invoice, serial and dates ready to share.
  • Export and privacy: you can get your data out, and it isn't used for ads.
  • Any currency and country, if you buy across borders.

Why we built TrackWarranty

Most people don't lose warranties because they lack storage. They lose them because nobody reminds them in time. Quick answers

What is the best app to keep track of warranties?

It depends on what you need. For reminders before expiry with offline storage and no sign-up, TrackWarranty. For receipts plus shopping deals, MrReceipt. For a simple cloud list, Warranty Keeper.

Is there a free warranty tracker app?

Yes. TrackWarranty is free on Android, and several others have free versions.

Can I just keep receipts in Google Drive?

You can, but you won't get reminders before warranties end, and searching scanned bills is slow. A warranty tracker adds expiry dates, reminders and claim details.
